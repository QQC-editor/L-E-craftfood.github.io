About Us

Welcome to L&E Craft Food! We are two women who share a deep love for Asian food and flavors, and we are thrilled to bring authentic, Asian-style craft food right to your table.

Lu spent over a decade living in China, where she developed a passion for handmade kimchi and exploring great food. El, originally from China, has always loved creative tofu dishes. After moving to the UK, she found it surprisingly hard to find interesting tofu products, which inspired her goal: to bring exciting, innovative tofu snacks to the market.

As co-founders, we are both deeply passionate about authentic Asian flavors and can't wait to share them with everyone here!
